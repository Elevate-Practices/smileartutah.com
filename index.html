Meet your dentist

Twenty years of smiles. Still learning.

Dr. Taylor Workman graduated from the University of Illinois Chicago College of Dentistry in 2005. He spent ten years building a practice in Broomfield, Colorado, where he found his calling in cosmetic dentistry, then three years practicing on New Zealand's North Island.

Questions patients ask

Are you accepting new patients?

Yes. New patients get a comprehensive exam, cleaning and X-rays for $99. Book online or call (435) 900-2450.

Is the cosmetic consultation free?

Yes. Your cosmetic consultation with Dr. Workman is complimentary, with no obligation.

Do you take my insurance?

We work with most major PPO plans, including Aetna, Cigna, Delta Dental, GEHA, Guardian, MetLife and UnitedHealthcare. Call us and we'll check your benefits for free.

What does "one patient at a time" mean?

Dr. Workman doesn't double-book or rush between rooms. When you're here, you have his full attention.

Monday through Thursday, 7:00 AM to 2:00 PM. We're closed Friday through Sunday.
