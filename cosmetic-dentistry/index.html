Why patients choose cosmetic dentistry

A smile you're proud of changes how you show up. Whether you want a subtle refresh or a complete makeover, Dr. Workman combines artistry, advanced materials and a one-patient-at-a-time approach for results that feel like you.

Our approach

  • Natural, lifelike results, never one-size-fits-all
  • Minimally invasive: healthy tooth structure is kept wherever possible
  • Master ceramist labs and careful bonding for results that last
  • A preview of your new smile before you commit

Common questions

How do I choose a cosmetic dentist?

Look for extensive cosmetic training, photos of real patients and reviews you trust, then meet the dentist. Your consultation with Dr. Workman is complimentary.

Do cosmetic procedures hurt?

Most are comfortable, with numbing when needed. Many, like whitening and bonding, need little or none.

How long do cosmetic results last?

Porcelain veneers often last 10 to 15 years or more; bonding 5 to 10 years; whitening 1 to 3 years, with touch-ups.

Can I see my new smile before treatment?

Yes. We use photos, 3D scans and try-in previews so you can approve the design first.
