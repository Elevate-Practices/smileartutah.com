Patient FAQs

What should I bring to my first appointment?

A photo ID, your insurance card and a list of your medications. Arriving 15 minutes early helps with check-in.

Which insurance plans do you accept?

We work with most major PPO plans, including Aetna, Anthem, Cigna, Delta Dental, GEHA, Guardian, MetLife, United Concordia and UnitedHealthcare. Call us and we'll check your plan.

How do I schedule or cancel an appointment?

Book online or call (435) 900-2450. Please give us 24 hours' notice if you need to cancel.

What payment options are available?

Insurance, CareCredit, Cherry, LendingClub and Proceed Finance, FSA and HSA cards, cash, check and major credit cards.
