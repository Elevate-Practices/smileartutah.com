Composite bonding in St. George, Utah

Sculpted, shade-matched composite that fixes chips, gaps and stains, often in one visit and without removing healthy tooth.

Book a visit(435) 900-2450

SmileArt Utah offers composite bonding at our studio in St. George, Utah, with Dr. Taylor Workman, DDS. Call (435) 900-2450 or book online.

What bonding is

Composite bonding is tooth-colored resin that Dr. Workman sculpts directly onto your teeth, layer by layer, to repair chips, close small gaps and cover stains.

How it works

  1. A consultation about your goals
  2. Shade selection to match your natural teeth
  3. Little or no preparation: the surface is gently roughened
  4. Artistic layering to recreate natural anatomy and translucency
  5. Each layer is hardened with a curing light
  6. Final shaping and a high-gloss polish

Bonding or veneers?

Bonding costs less, needs no tooth reduction and is often done in one visit, which makes it ideal for small fixes. Porcelain veneers last longer and resist stains better for full makeovers. Dr. Workman will tell you which fits.

Common questions

How much does bonding cost?

Composite bonding typically costs $300 to $600 per tooth, depending on the size of the repair.

How long does bonding last?

Usually 5 to 10 years with good care. Touch-ups are simple.

Does bonding stain?

Composite can pick up stains over time, more than porcelain. A polish at your cleanings helps.

Does bonding hurt?

Usually not. Most bonding needs no numbing.
