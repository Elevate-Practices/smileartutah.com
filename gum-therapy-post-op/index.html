After your visit

Gum therapy post-op

How to help your gums heal after your deep cleaning.

Book a visit(435) 900-2450

What to expect

You'll notice less redness, bleeding and swelling in your gums. Your teeth may feel smoother, and your mouth will taste and feel better. If we used anesthetic, your lips, teeth and tongue may be numb for several hours: don't chew until it wears off, so you don't bite or burn yourself.

Caring for your mouth

Good daily home care is key to healing. Use an electric toothbrush, floss and the rinse we recommend. If we prescribed 0.12% chlorhexidine rinse, use it as labeled. You can also rinse with warm salt water 2 or 3 times a day (1 teaspoon salt in 8 oz water). Brush gently.

Discomfort

If your doctor says it's safe for you, take an over-the-counter pain reliever such as ibuprofen, as directed on the label, before the anesthetic wears off. Extra sensitivity to hot, cold or sweets is normal while gums heal. A sensitivity toothpaste or fluoride rinse helps. If sensitivity is severe or doesn't improve, call us.

Bleeding

Some light bleeding may happen when you brush over the next few days; it should taper off after 2 or 3 days.

Eating

Eat soft foods for a few days and chew on the side opposite the treated area.

Smoking

Avoid smoking for 24 to 48 hours. Smoking slows healing and makes it harder to keep gums healthy. We recommend quitting.

Your part

We usually treat one half of the mouth per visit. Please keep every appointment to finish the other side and your maintenance visits after.
