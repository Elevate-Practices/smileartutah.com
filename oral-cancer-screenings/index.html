Preventive dentistry

Oral cancer screenings in St. George, Utah

A quick, painless check at every exam.

Book a visit(435) 900-2450

SmileArt Utah offers oral cancer screenings at our studio in St. George, Utah, with Dr. Taylor Workman, DDS. Call (435) 900-2450 or book online.

What we check

Dr. Workman looks and feels for changes in your lips, tongue, cheeks, throat and neck. It takes a few minutes and is part of every comprehensive exam.

Signs to watch for

  • A sore that doesn't heal in two weeks
  • A white or red patch
  • A lump or thickening
  • Trouble swallowing or a lasting sore throat
