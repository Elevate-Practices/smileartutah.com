We're glad you're considering SmileArt Utah. Here, every patient gets the attention, artistry and expertise they deserve, starting with your first visit.

Your first visit

  1. Book online or call (435) 900-2450. We'll find a time that works for you.
  2. Fill out your new patient forms before your visit, or arrive 15 minutes early.
  3. Dr. Workman personally does your exam, with digital X-rays and an oral cancer screening, and you get a thorough cleaning.
  4. We talk through what we found and make a plan for your goals and budget. No pressure.

What to bring

  • Photo ID
  • Your insurance card, if you have one
  • A list of your current medications
  • Recent dental X-rays, if you have them

Payment options

We work with most major PPO plans and offer CareCredit, Cherry, LendingClub and Proceed Finance, plus FSA and HSA cards, cash, check and all major credit cards. See insurance and financing.

New patient questions

What's included in the $99 visit?

A comprehensive exam with Dr. Workman, a routine cleaning and digital X-rays.

Will I get a cleaning at my first visit?

Yes. It's part of the $99 new-patient visit.

How long does the first appointment take?

Plan on about an hour to an hour and a half, so nothing is rushed.

Can I fill out my paperwork beforehand?

Yes. Ask us when you book, or arrive 15 minutes early to complete it in the office.

What if I need more than a cleaning?

Dr. Workman will explain what he sees, what can wait and what each option costs. No pressure.
