What veneers are

Porcelain veneers are thin ceramic shells, about 0.3 to 0.5 mm, bonded to the front of your teeth. They change color, shape, length and the look of alignment, and they resist stains.

Who they're for

  • Chipped, worn or uneven teeth
  • Stains whitening can't lift
  • Small gaps and minor crowding
  • Teeth that look short or small
  • Anyone who wants a natural-looking smile that lasts

How Dr. Workman designs your veneers

  1. A complimentary consultation about your goals
  2. Digital photos and a 3D scan of your teeth
  3. A try-in preview, so you see your new smile before you commit
  4. Conservative preparation (often 0.3–0.5 mm of enamel)
  5. Temporary veneers to test-drive length, shape and shade for 1–2 weeks
  6. Final veneers made by a master ceramist
  7. Bonding under rubber-dam isolation and a careful bite check

Why the details matter

The difference between a smile that looks done and one that looks like yours is artistry, technique and materials. Dr. Workman works with top ceramist labs, bonds every veneer under rubber-dam isolation, and plans the shape around your face, not a template.

Common questions

How much do porcelain veneers cost in St. George?

Porcelain veneers typically cost $1,200 to $2,500 per tooth, depending on the case and materials. You'll get an exact quote at your complimentary consultation, and financing is available.

How long do veneers last?

With good care, porcelain veneers often last 10 to 15 years or more. Regular cleanings and a night guard, if you grind, help them last.

Do veneers ruin your natural teeth?

No. Most veneers need only a thin layer of enamel, far less than a crown. Some cases need little or no preparation at all.

Do veneers look natural?

They should. Dr. Workman designs the shape and shade around your face and features, and a ceramist adds the translucency and texture of real enamel.

Does getting veneers hurt?

Most patients say it feels like a routine filling. We numb the area and check in with you throughout.
